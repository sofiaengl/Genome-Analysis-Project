# Genome-Analysis-Project

Uppsala Universitet, Genome Analysis VT26

This project focuses on analysis of genomic and transcriptomic data of the vancomycin-resistant bacterium *Enterococcus faecium* from the article *"RNA-seq and Tn-seq reveal fitness determinants of vancomycin-resistant Enterococcus faecium during growth in human serum"* Zhang X, De Maat V, Prieto AMG, Prajsnar TK, Bayjanov JR, De Been M, Rogers MRC, Bonten MJM, Mesnage S, Willems RJL, Van Schaik W. 2017. BMC Genomics 18: 893. This project will reanalyse the data from the study using bioinformatic analyses (see the project pipeline in "Project Plan" and all the steps in wiki) to investigate genes that are involved in the growth and survival of the bacteria during growth in human serum versus in rich medium for future research and treatments of the infections caused by the bacterium. 

This project found that the purine metabolism genes purD, purH and purL was upregulated and highly expressed during growth in human serum compared to rich medium. This aligns with the findings of the paper where they found through Tn-seq those genes being required for growth in serum and through RNA-seq those genes being significantly upregulated during growth in serum.
